A real LCS-based diff, not a character-by-character guess. Comparison is capped at 1,200 units per side for browser performance.

How to use Text Comparer / Diff

  1. Paste the original text on the left.
  2. Paste the changed text on the right.
  3. Review highlighted additions and removals.

Example

Input
Left: The cat sat Right: The dog sat
Output
- The cat sat + The dog sat

Frequently asked questions

How large a text can I compare?

Up to 1,200 lines or words per side. Larger inputs show a clear error instead of freezing your browser.
