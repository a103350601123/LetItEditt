      In-depth guide
      
Units, styles and formats

      
Paragraphs contain several sentences, sentences are single blocks, words are sliced to an exact count, and list items can be plain bullets, HTML lists or Markdown bullets. Classic style chooses random words from a 179-word vocabulary; standard style reuses fixed passages.

      
HTML output can wrap paragraphs in paragraph tags, and list output can use ordered or unordered HTML lists. Preview and Source tabs show the rendered and raw versions respectively.

      
Why the caps exist

      
The caps mirror the supplied reference tool and keep generation responsive. They are documented here rather than hidden, because the reference page advertises unlimited output. Choose the largest permitted amount for long mock-ups, or run the generator more than once.

  





  

    

      
Frequently asked questions

      

  Is there really no limit?
  
No. The reference description says there is no limit, but the application caps output at 100 paragraphs, 500 sentences, 5,000 words or 100 list items depending on the unit.



  Can I get an exact word count?
  
Yes. Choose the Words unit and enter the number you need. The generator slices to exactly that many words.



  What is the difference between classic and standard?
  
Classic builds sentences from a pool of Latin-like words and avoids repeats within a sentence. Standard cycles the four classic Lorem ipsum sentences, optionally starting partway through the passage.
