Extract Emails from Text

Unicode-aware email detection that removes duplicates and returns one address per line.

How to use Extract Emails from Text

  1. Paste text containing email addresses.
  2. Unique matches appear automatically.
  3. Copy the list.

Example

Input
Contact a@b.com or c@d.org
Output
a@b.com c@d.org

Frequently asked questions

Are duplicates removed?

Yes. Addresses are unique and keep their first-seen order.
