Free online PDF editor

Use the LetItEdit PDF editor to change a PDF in your browser without installing anything. Your file stays on your device.

Open the PDF editor

What you can do

  • Add text with a choice of fonts, colours and sizes, and resize it with the mouse or your finger
  • Add pictures such as a logo or a signature image
  • Whiteout old text and type the correct text in the closest matching font
  • Delete, keep and rotate pages, and add another PDF to the end
  • Add page numbers and a text watermark

How to use it

  1. Open the PDF editor and choose your PDF.
  2. Use Add text, Add picture or Whiteout and replace, then tap the page.
  3. Press Apply edits to the PDF, then Download PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

You can cover old text with Whiteout and type new text on top. The old text may still exist inside the file, so do not use it to hide private data.

Can I merge PDF files?

Yes. Add another PDF to the end, or use the File merger to combine several PDFs in the order you choose.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark unless you add one.
