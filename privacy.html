Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Free tools

The free editors run in your browser. Your image, PDF, Word or Excel file is not uploaded to LetItEdit. The text extractor downloads language data from a content network, but your image stays on your device.

Orders

When you hire a freelancer, LetItEdit collects your name, your email or WhatsApp number, your order details and the file you upload. It uses them only to take payment, complete your order and contact you about it.

Services used

Cloudflare hosts the website. Supabase stores orders and uploaded files. PayPal processes payments, and LetItEdit never sees your card details. An email service sends order notifications to LetItEdit.

Retention

Uploaded files are deleted 30 days after an order is completed. You can ask for your order data to be deleted at any time.

Storage in your browser

The site saves your unfinished order number in your browser so you can resume it. It does not use advertising cookies.

Email hello@letitedit.com or message LetItEdit on WhatsApp.
