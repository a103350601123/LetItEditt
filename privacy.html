Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What LetItEdit collects

Your name, your email address or WhatsApp number (you only need to give one), the instructions you write, and the files you upload. Payments are handled by PayPal. LetItEdit never sees or stores your card details.

How LetItEdit uses it

Only to complete your order: confirming payment, editing your file, delivering it and giving support.

Storage and deletion

Orders and files are stored with Supabase, a cloud database provider. LetItEdit deletes uploaded files 30 days after your order is complete.

Sharing

LetItEdit does not sell or share your information. It is only handled by services needed to run the site and take payment, such as Supabase and PayPal.
