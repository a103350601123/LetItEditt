Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Collection

For our free tools, your files are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers. For freelance orders, we collect your name, contact method (email or WhatsApp), project instructions, and the files you upload. Payments are handled securely by PayPal.

Storage & Deletion

Freelance order data and uploaded files are stored in secure cloud buckets (Supabase). Files are permanently deleted 30 days after your order is completed.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or uploaded files with any third parties.
