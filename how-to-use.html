How to use LetItEdit

Two ways to get your file edited: do it yourself with the free tools, or hire a freelance editor.

Free tools

  1. Open Free tools and choose a tool.
  2. Choose a file from your device. It opens in the editor straight away.
  3. Edit it, then download the result. Your file stays on your device.

Image editor

Pick a tool on the left: Draw, Highlight, Arrow, Box, Text, Step number, Crop, Blur area or Pixelate area. Drag on the image to use it. Use Undo and Redo, the zoom buttons, and the sliders to adjust the whole image. Choose PNG, JPG or WebP and press Download image.

Text extractor

Choose an image, pick the language of the text, and wait for the result. Edit the text if needed, then copy it or download it as a .txt file.

PDF editor

Open a PDF, then rotate pages, delete pages (for example 2,5-7), keep only some pages, add another PDF to the end, add page numbers or a watermark. Press Download PDF when you are done.

Word editor

Open a .docx file, click in the page to edit the text, use the bold, italic, underline and list buttons, then press Download .docx or Print or save as PDF.

Spreadsheet editor

Open an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file, pick a sheet tab, click a cell to edit it (formulas start with =), then press Download .xlsx.

Hire a freelancer

  1. Open Hire a freelancer, choose a service and delivery time.
  2. Press Continue to details. Enter your name, your email or WhatsApp number (only one), and what you need done.
  3. Tick the box to accept the Terms of Service, then press Proceed to payment.
  4. Pay with PayPal in the pop-up. Keep your order number.
  5. Wait for approval. Your order page updates by itself and asks you to upload your file.
  6. Upload your file. The edited file is sent to your email or WhatsApp.

If something goes wrong

Check your internet connection first. If you paid and closed the page, open the order page again and press Resume order. Still stuck? Message LetItEdit on WhatsApp with your order number.
