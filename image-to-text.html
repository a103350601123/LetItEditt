Image to text converter (OCR)

The LetItEdit text extractor reads the text in a picture and gives it back as text you can edit, copy and save. It works with photos, screenshots, scanned pages and receipts.

Open the text extractor

What you can do

  • Extract text from images in more than 90 languages, including English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Spanish and French
  • Choose Receipt or table mode to keep columns, prices and currency symbols in line
  • Handle right-to-left languages such as Arabic, Urdu and Hebrew
  • Save the result as Word, PDF, Excel, CSV, web page, Markdown or plain text
  • Copy the text with one button

How to use it

  1. Open the text extractor and choose the image.
  2. Press Languages and tick every language that appears in the document.
  3. Check the text, choose a format and press Save text.

Frequently asked questions

Can it read a receipt or bill?

Yes. Choose Receipt or table and save the result as Excel or CSV. Always check numbers and currency symbols, because OCR can make mistakes on blurry pictures.

Which languages are supported?

More than 90, including English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Persian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish, French and German.

Is handwriting supported?

OCR works best on printed text. Handwriting is often read poorly.
