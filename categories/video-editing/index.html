Video

Video Editing

Trim, crop, resize or combine video in your browser without uploading it. The published Video Trimmer cuts a section out of one file, the Video Cropper keeps a chosen region of the frame, the Video Resizer re-encodes a clip to a new resolution, and the Video Merger joins several clips into one MP4 or WebM file with adjustable output settings.

About Video Editing

A home for guides and browser-side tools for trimming, cropping, subtitling and exporting video.

This category is part of the permanent LetitEdit taxonomy. The published tool below is live; planned tools remain clearly labelled.

Published tools

Live

These tools work in your browser. Each one has its own page, documentation and documented limits.

Video TrimmerTrim a video online for free. Set exact start and end times, keep or remove a section, preview the cut, and download a real MP4 or WebM file. Processing stays on your device. → Video MergerCombine multiple clips into one file. Upload, reorder and merge videos with adjustable aspect ratio, resolution, padding and quality. Real browser-side processing with no upload. → Video CropperCrop a video to any region or aspect ratio. Drag the crop frame, use 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and other presets, preview the result, then export a real MP4 or WebM file on your device. → Video ResizerResize a video to 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, 240p or exact custom dimensions. Fit or fill the frame, choose a background, keep the audio, and export a real MP4 or WebM file locally. →
