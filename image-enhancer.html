Frequently asked questions

Is this an AI upscaler?

No. It uses professional resizing, noise reduction and sharpening. It makes a picture larger and cleaner, but it cannot add real detail that is missing from a very small picture.

Can it remove any background?

It works best on plain backgrounds such as white or a single colour. It is not an AI cut-out for busy photos.

Which formats can I upload?

Common image formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and HEIC.
