Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about the category structure, privacy and how future browser-side tools will be added. Questions expand on click or with the keyboard.

About this site

Are any tools available right now?

No. The tool library was removed so the site can be rebuilt around a smaller category structure. The category pages, blog, news and FAQ are the published content at the moment.

Do I need an account?

No. The published pages are static content and do not ask for an account, sign-up or subscription.

Privacy and safety

Do these pages upload my text or files?

The published category, blog, news, FAQ and policy pages do not provide an input for text or files, so nothing is uploaded from them. Future tool pages must explain exactly what happens to any input before you use them.

Are you tracking visitors?

The content-only site uses local styles and system fonts. It does not load advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. A hosting provider may keep ordinary server access logs for security and troubleshooting.

Site structure

Why are the category pages empty of tools?

The nine categories are the permanent taxonomy. They exist so future guides and tools have stable homes without presenting unfinished or unsupported functionality as available.

How will new tools be added later?

A tool will be introduced only with a real page, documented behaviour, honest limits and working checks. The site generator will then add its listing, metadata and sitemap entry; no placeholder tool is published.

Accessibility

Do you support mobile screens and non-English text?

The published pages are responsive and use system font fallbacks with Unicode-aware HTML. Each page declares its language and direction, so left-to-right and right-to-left content can be added without changing the theme.
