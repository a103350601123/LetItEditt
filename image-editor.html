Free online image editor

LetItEdit has a free online image editor that works in your browser on a computer or a phone. Open a photo or screenshot, mark it up, and download it in the same quality. Your picture stays on your device.

Open the image editor

What you can do

  • Draw, highlight and add arrows, boxes and numbered steps to a screenshot
  • Blur or pixelate passwords, faces and account numbers before you share an image
  • Add text that you can move, stretch and recolour at any time
  • Crop, rotate, flip and adjust brightness, contrast and colour
  • Resize an image by width and height, or shrink it to a target file size such as 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB

How to use it

  1. Open the image editor and choose your picture.
  2. Pick a tool such as Arrow, Text or Blur area and use it on the picture.
  3. Press Download image, or use Resize and file size to reach a smaller file.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compress an image to 20 KB?

Yes. Open the image, type a target size such as 20 and choose KB, then press Preview result. JPG or WebP give the best quality at small sizes.

Does the editor upload my picture?

No. The editor runs in your browser, so your picture is not sent to a server.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Use one finger to draw and two fingers to zoom and move the picture.
