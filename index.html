Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

How does ordering work?

Choose a service and speed, enter your details, and pay through PayPal. LetItEdit confirms your payment by email, and this page then asks you to upload your file. The edited file is sent to your email or WhatsApp.

How long does payment confirmation take?

Payments are confirmed by hand, usually within 15 minutes during working hours. If it takes longer, message LetItEdit on WhatsApp with your order number.

Is my file private?

Yes. Your file is stored in private cloud storage and used only for your order. LetItEdit does not share or sell it, and deletes it 30 days after the order is complete.

What if changes are needed?

Revisions are free. Reply to the delivery email or WhatsApp message within 48 hours and LetItEdit will fix it.

Which file types are accepted?

  • PDF formatting: .pdf files such as resumes, contracts and reports
  • Word documents: .doc and .docx files such as theses and manuscripts
  • Excel sheets: .xls and .xlsx files such as data sheets and models
  • Screenshots: .png, .jpg and .jpeg images

Is there another way to pay?

PayPal is the main method. If you need another option, message LetItEdit on WhatsApp before you order.
