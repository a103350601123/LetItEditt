Read the full FAQ
What is LetitEdit?

LetitEdit is a collection of free online tools for editing images, video, audio and screen recordings. The library is organised into 28 categories, with 13 tools published so far.

Are the tools free?

Yes. The published tools are free to use and do not require a subscription or payment.

Do I need an account?

No. You can open any published tool and start working without signing up or logging in.

Can I use LetitEdit on mobile?

Yes. The homepage and published tools are responsive and work in modern mobile browsers. Larger video jobs may be limited by your device's memory and browser support.

Are my files uploaded?

The published editing tools are designed to process your files locally in your browser. They do not send your media to a LetitEdit server. Each tool page explains its own processing, and the hosting provider may keep ordinary access logs for the pages themselves.

Which tools are available?

There are 13 live tools: an image editor and image compressor; a video editor, trimmer, merger, cropper, resizer, compressor and converter; an audio trimmer, merger and converter; and a screen recorder. New tools are added when they are ready.

Are there PDF tools?

Not yet. PDF and document editing is one of the 28 categories, but no PDF tool is published at the moment. The category page will list tools when they are ready.
