Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information LetitEdit collects and how our browser-side tools handle your content.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Text and files

Our text and developer tools run locally in your browser. Text you type or paste is processed in memory and is not transmitted by our tool code to a LetitEdit server. We do not store your text in a database or account.

Logs and analytics

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers or analytics scripts on LetitEdit pages. A hosting provider may keep ordinary server access logs such as IP address, browser user agent and requested URL for security and troubleshooting.

Third-party resources

Some preserved legacy tools may request fonts or libraries from third-party providers such as Google Fonts. Those providers may receive standard web request data such as your IP address. Our generated tools use local scripts and system fonts and do not require external processing services.

Clipboard and downloads

Copy and download buttons act only after you click them. Clipboard access is handled by the browser and is used solely to copy your own output.

Children’s privacy

LetitEdit is a general-audience tool site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to info@letitedit.com.
