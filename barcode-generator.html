Frequently asked questions

Do EAN-13 and UPC need a check digit?

No. Type 12 digits for EAN-13 or 11 digits for UPC-A and the check digit is added for you.

PNG or SVG?

Use SVG for printing at any size and PNG for screens.

Can I scan it before printing?

Yes. Always test with a scanner before printing many copies.
