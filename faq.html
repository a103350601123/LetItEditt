Frequently asked questions

Answers about the free online editors and hiring a freelance editor.

Are the free tools really free and private?

Yes. The image editor, text extractor, PDF editor, Word editor and spreadsheet editor are free, need no account and add no watermark. They run in your browser, so your file is not uploaded to LetItEdit.

Which file types can I open?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP), PDF files, Word .docx files, and Excel .xlsx, .xls and .csv files. Older .doc files are not supported by the Word editor.

What can the image editor do?

Draw, highlight, add arrows, boxes, text and numbered steps, crop, rotate, flip, blur or pixelate private details, adjust brightness and contrast, and download as PNG, JPG or WebP.

How accurate is the text extractor?

It uses OCR and works best on clear, straight, high-contrast images. Always check the result for mistakes, especially numbers and names.

What can the PDF editor do?

Merge PDFs, delete or extract pages, rotate pages, add page numbers and add a text watermark. It does not rewrite existing text inside a PDF.

Will my Word or Excel formatting change?

The Word editor keeps the page layout when you open a file. A downloaded .docx is a best-effort copy, so use Print or save as PDF for an exact copy. The spreadsheet editor keeps formulas, but cell colours may be simplified when saving.

How does hiring a freelancer work?

Choose a service and delivery time, enter your details and accept the terms, then pay with PayPal. LetItEdit confirms your payment by email approval, your order page then asks for your file, and you receive the edited file by email or WhatsApp.

How long does payment approval take?

Payments are approved by hand, usually within a few minutes during working hours. Your order page updates by itself when approved. If you wait longer than 30 minutes, message LetItEdit on WhatsApp with your order number.

Do I need both an email and a WhatsApp number?

No. Give only one. LetItEdit uses it to send your finished file and to contact you about your order.

What are the prices?

Screenshot editing and annotating is $1 for 6 to 12 hours, $2 for 4 hours, $3 for 2 hours and $5 for 30 minutes to 1 hour. Other services have their own prices on the Hire a freelancer page.

What if I need changes?

Revisions are free. Reply to the delivery message within 48 hours and LetItEdit will fix the issue.

Can LetItEdit edit IDs, certificates or receipts?

No. LetItEdit does not edit documents to forge or mislead, such as IDs, certificates, receipts, bank statements or medical records. See the Terms of Service.

What happens if my internet disconnects?

The site shows an offline message and tools and orders pause until you reconnect. If you already paid, return to the order page and tap Resume order.
