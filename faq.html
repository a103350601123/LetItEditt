Frequently Asked Questions

How does ordering work?

Choose a service and speed, enter your details, and pay through PayPal. LetItEdit confirms your payment by email, then you upload your file. The edited file is sent by email or WhatsApp.

How long does payment confirmation take?

Usually within 15 minutes during working hours. If it takes longer, message LetItEdit on WhatsApp with your order number.

Is my file private?

Yes. Files are stored in private cloud storage, used only for your order, and deleted 30 days after completion.

What if changes are needed?

Revisions are free. Reply to the delivery email or WhatsApp message within 48 hours.
