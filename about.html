About LetItEdit

Freelance document and video editing

LetItEdit is run by a freelance editor based in Pakistan. LetItEdit formats PDFs, Word documents, Excel sheets and screenshots, and edits short videos, for students, job seekers and small businesses.

How LetItEdit works

Each job is edited by hand and checked before delivery. If something is not right, LetItEdit fixes it for free.

Why you pay first

Payment reserves time in the schedule so rush jobs can be delivered quickly. LetItEdit verifies each payment by email. Once it is confirmed, you can upload your file.
